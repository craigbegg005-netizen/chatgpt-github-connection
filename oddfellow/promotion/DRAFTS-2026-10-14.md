## Posting guidance (for the owner)
1. **Reddit:** post only where self-promotion is allowed (check sidebar rules; weekly promo threads are the safe default). The drafts above are written to lead with the format and invite feedback, which survives most rules — but a ban is a real cost, so when in doubt, skip the sub.
2. **X:** one product per day, not all three at once. Paycheck bundle first (highest price, broadest audience).
3. **Where a real account history exists, use it** — a first-ever post that is an ad underperforms. These are written to also work as comments/replies.
4. **Never add urgency or claims not on the listing** ("changed my life", "limited time", income promises). The listings are honest; the promotion stays honest or it costs the store.
5. Track what gets posted where; if one listing sells, the delivery-verification checklist (BSI-COMMERCE-002) becomes the next job.
