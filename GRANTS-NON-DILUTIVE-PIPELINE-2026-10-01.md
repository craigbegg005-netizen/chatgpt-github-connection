## Hard rules for this lane

1. **Zero-spend first.** No application fee, no upfront cost. If a program requires payment to apply, it is out of scope unless Craig authorizes it.
2. **Never fabricate** business eligibility, ownership, revenue, employee counts, certifications, degrees, demographic qualifications, matching funds, research credentials, or registrations. A fabricated eligibility claim on a federal application is a legal exposure, not a shortcut.
3. **Do not frame normal application development as scientific R&D.** NSF states this explicitly in its own pitch instructions. Oddfellow's *verification architecture* may be genuine R&D; shipping the app is not.
