## The gap this identifies

The demand evidence points at **"botanical gallery wall set"** — a *set* of coordinated prints,
not a single poster. Our prepared poster is one piece. The 2-3-5 composition guidance and the
"buyers who like one print often buy several" observation both favour a set, which would raise
average order value without new artwork categories.

That is a proposal for the owner, not a unilateral change: it needs new artwork, and artwork
creation is currently blocked (see inventory doc).
