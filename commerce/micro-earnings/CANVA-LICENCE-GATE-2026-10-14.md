# Canva licence gate — what the three products must satisfy before sale

**Date:** 2026-10-14 · **Lane:** micro-earnings Gate 2 · **Sources:** Canva's Content License
Agreement, "Using Canva to create products for sale", and "Licensing explained" (all linked below).

This converts a previously vague "unverified resale-rights risk" into **specific pass/fail
conditions** that can be checked against the actual files in minutes.

## The single rule that governs this lane

> **"You can only sell original designs. You can't sell any Canva content on a standalone basis."**

And Canva defines *standalone* precisely:

> "Using the Content in its original form, solely using a filter, changing colors, resizing,
> cropping, or outlining the Content, **or using a single piece of Content with minimal additional
> design elements such as a border** is a standalone use."

## Directly analogous worked example from Canva — the good news

Canva's own help page gives this pair, and it is almost exactly our case:

| Example | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A sticker sheet of **20 individual library elements**, downloaded as a flattened PDF | ❌ **NOT allowed** — standalone use of each element, and individual downloads resemble selling clipart |
| A sticker sheet of **20 original quotes he wrote**, typed out using **Canva fonts** | ✅ **ALLOWED** |

**Our three products are in the second category if — and only if — their substance is original
authored text.** All three are written content in a Canva layout:

- **15-Minute Home Rescue Cards** — eight routines, timings, stop and sufficiency conditions → original writing
- **No-Reply Follow-Up Kit** — five message templates, timing guide, signal checklist, contact log → original writing
- **Service Quote Builder** — quote worksheet, pricing formula, pre-send checklist → original writing

**So the likely answer is: compliant, provided the original text is the substance and no single
Canva library image is doing the design work.**

## 🔴 The three conditions that could still block, and they are real

**1. Canva templates containing Pro Content may NOT be sold as PDFs — at all.**
> "You can't sell templates with Pro Content as a PDF or other file types, **whether flattened or not**."

⚠️ **This is the sharpest risk.** Two of the three products were *described* as "cards" and a
"template". If any was built from a **Canva template that contains Pro Content**, the PDF sale is
prohibited regardless of how much original text was added. **Check the design type first.**

**2. Unedited Pro Content in a digital publication must stay under 480,000 pixels.**
> "If you're using Pro Content in an unedited format (which means a photo by itself on a page with
> no elements overlaid on the top) you have to stick to the maximum 480,000 pixel limit."

Practical meaning: a full-page Pro stock photo with a heading on top is **not** "unedited" — but a
bare photo page is, and must be ≤480,000 px (e.g. 800×600). **Check any image-only page.**

**3. Pro Content must be placed using Canva — not imported into another tool.**
> "You can't download Pro Content from Canva and then use it to design your e-book in another
> software application."

If any page was finished outside Canva, that page's Pro Content usage is out of licence.

## The check, in order — five minutes with the files open

| # | Check | Fail looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Was it built from a Canva template containing Pro Content?** | Yes → **do not sell as PDF** |
| 2 | **Is the substance original authored text?** | A page whose design work is a Canva illustration |
| 3 | **Any page that is a bare Pro photo/illustration?** | Yes → must be ≤480,000 px |
| 4 | **Was any Pro Content placed outside Canva?** | Yes → that usage is unlicensed |
| 5 | **Free-user watermarks anywhere?** | Yes → unlicensed Pro Content, remove or license it |

Checks 1 and 3 are the ones most likely to bite.

## What I still cannot do without the files

**I cannot run any of these checks.** The PDFs are not on this sandbox and the Canva designs
require a sign-in. So this document is a **prepared gate**, not a passed one. It becomes
actionable the moment the files are accessible.

**If the check passes:** the products are sellable as original written works, and the residual
concern drops to nothing more than the ordinary accuracy of the copy.
**If check 1 fails:** they need rebuilding from a scratch design, not from a template — which is a
contained fix, not a lost cause.
