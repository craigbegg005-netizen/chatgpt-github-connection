## 🔴 PREPRESS — the finding now applies to the WHOLE collection, not one SKU

**All six are 1122 × 1402 px** — identical to the figure in the handoff.

At 300 DPI that is **3.74 × 4.67 inches**, which is **smaller than Fourthwall's smallest poster
(5×7)**. Full analysis: `commerce/prepress/SAMURAI-PREPRESS-2026-10-14.md`.

| Size | Real DPI | Verdict |

| **8×10** | **140** (exact 4:5 aspect) | ⚠️ soft |
| 11×14 | 100 | ❌ poor |
| 16×20 | 70 | ❌ unusable |
| 24×36 | 39 | ❌ unusable |

**My earlier warning is now confirmed, not hypothetical:** I flagged on 2026-10-14 that *"if the

that would be a 6.4× linear upscale of a file with 39 real DPI.
